Budgetly

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short: Budgetly keeps your budget on your iPhone. If you create an account, it is also stored in our cloud database so it syncs and you can connect AI assistants. We don't sell your data, show ads or track you across other apps, and you can delete everything at any time from the app.

What we collect

  • Account details (only if you create an account): your email address, and your name and profile photo if you sign in with Google or Apple.
  • Budget data you enter: transactions (amount, date, category, note), categories and monthly budgets, recurring expenses, savings goals, and your currency and time zone.
  • AI assistant connections: when you connect ChatGPT, Claude, Codex or another assistant, we store a hashed version of the private link and when it was last used.

We do not collect your location, contacts, bank login details, advertising identifiers or analytics about how you use the app.

How we use it

  • To show your budget and sync it between your devices.
  • To let AI assistants you connect read and update your budget, only through a link you create.
  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.

Where it is stored

Without an account, your data stays only on your iPhone. With an account, it is also stored with our database provider, Supabase, on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Data is encrypted in transit, and access is limited so that each account can only read its own data.

AI assistants

Connecting an AI assistant is optional. When you do, that assistant can read and change your Budgetly data through the link you created, and its provider processes what it reads under its own privacy policy. You can revoke a link at any time in Profile → AI Assistants.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your data. We share it only with the service providers needed to run Budgetly (Supabase for the database and sign-in, Google or Apple if you choose to sign in with them), or if the law requires it.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account and all its data from our servers and your iPhone.
  • Log out: Profile → Log out removes your data from the iPhone; it stays in your account.
  • Access or correct: you can see and edit all your data in the app, or contact us.

If you live in the EU, UK or Israel, you also have the right to request a copy of your data, object to processing, and complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Budgetly is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
